# Historical-Jurisdiction Records-Holder Capacity

## Purpose

`authority-registry.3` separates an institution's present-day `geographicScope` from its evidence-holding capacity for a historical jurisdiction. A capacity is immutable Registry metadata and does not itself establish claim authority, perspective sufficiency, or an admissible historical fact.

## Governing constraints

- Exact historical-jurisdiction matching only; `International`, present geography, and successor-state names are not aliases.
- A capacity is bounded by jurisdiction, period, domain, collection, record type, permitted research roles, subject relationship, provenance, and access limitations.
- Topic Authority Bindings must select the capacity identity explicitly. A selected capacity is only an acquisition-availability proof; normal claim-relative authority, source validation, independence, conflict, coverage, and perspective audits still apply.
- Missing capacity fails closed and persists structured requirement/candidate/rejection lineage in the terminal failure record.

## Initial governed admission: State Archive of the Russian Federation (GARF)

The initial `Soviet Union` capacity is deliberately narrow: 1945–1951 Soviet Military Administration in Germany (SVAG) records and related official documentation, for Soviet occupation administration and policy in occupied Germany. It does not declare GARF authoritative for all Soviet history.

The admission is supported by GARF's official site:

- [GARF official institutional description](https://statearchive.ru/) identifies it as a federal archive and successor to central USSR/Russian state-authority archives.
- [GARF fonds description](https://statearchive.ru/371) describes its USSR central-government holdings.
- [GARF SVAG electronic archive](https://statearchive.ru/384) exposes the bounded collection context.
- [GARF SVAG collection description](https://statearchive.ru/397) describes the Soviet Military Administration in Germany, including Soviet-zone and Berlin administrative records.

The Registry entry records Russian-language and reading-room/request access limitations. The system must not infer future availability or claim relevance from this admission.
